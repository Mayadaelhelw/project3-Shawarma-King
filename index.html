Grill News

Chicken Shawarma with Bell Peppers

Fresh & Juicy

Fresh chicken pieces seasoned with the finest blend of special Eastern spices, carefully cooked to retain their juice and flavor. Fresh bell pepper slices are added for a touch of sweetness and crunch. The shawarma is served in soft Arabic bread, with creamy yogurt sauce, fresh tomatoes, and lettuce, for a complete taste experience that takes you straight to the heart of Eastern cuisine.

Grilled Cheese Stuffed Kofta

Cheesy Delight

Kofta rich with fresh, carefully minced meat, stuffed with melted cheese to give you a unique taste experience. Grilled over charcoal to release a tempting aroma, crispy on the outside and tender on the inside. Perfect for fans of rich and diverse flavors, it can be served with spicy tomato sauce or yogurt sauce to complete the meal perfectly.

Honey Lemon Shish Tawook

Sweet & Tangy

Medium-sized chicken pieces marinated in a special blend of fresh honey and natural lemon juice, giving the meat a balanced sweet and tangy flavor. Grilled over charcoal to create a delicious golden crust while keeping the inside juicy. Perfect with hot Arabic bread and a tomato and lettuce salad, making a complete meal that combines great taste with an appetizing presentation.

Grilled Aleppo Sausage

Spicy & Smoky

Traditional Aleppo sausage made from the finest fresh meats and special spices, giving you an irresistible rich flavor. It is charcoal-grilled to acquire a distinctive smoky taste while retaining its natural juiciness in every bite. Served with fresh Arabic bread and seasonal vegetables, making it an ideal choice for meat lovers and fans of authentic Eastern flavors.
